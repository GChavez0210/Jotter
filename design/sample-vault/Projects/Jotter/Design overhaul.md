Jotter should feel like a writing tool, not a settings panel. This note tracks the visual overhaul. Daily progress lives in [[2026-10-19]].

> [!note] Direction
> Warm paper, one ink accent, and type that does the hierarchy. Chrome sits flat and stays quiet until you need it.

## Principles

- The note is the interface. Hairlines split regions, not floating cards.
- One accent, used for the active item, links, selection and the primary button.
- Typography carries hierarchy. UI text at 13 px, note text at 17 px.
- Tools appear when needed. Formatting shows on selection; rare actions live in the palette.
